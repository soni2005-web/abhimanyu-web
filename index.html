FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Important details to understand before starting a website project.

How much does a website cost?

Starting packages are ₹1,500 for a one-page website, ₹3,000 for a business website and ₹7,000 for an advanced website. Your final price depends on the agreed features, content, pages and requirements.

Can you build a website for my shop?

Yes. You can discuss a website for a shop, local business, service provider, company, portfolio or landing page. The exact design and features will be agreed before work starts.

Will my website appear on Google?

The website can be prepared with basic SEO elements. Google decides independently whether and when to index a website and where it appears in results. Search visibility and ranking cannot be guaranteed.

Are domain and hosting included?

Not automatically. Paid domain registration, hosting, maintenance and advanced features may involve additional costs. These should be discussed before the project starts.
