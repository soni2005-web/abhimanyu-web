Common questions

Good to know before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Packages have the starting prices shown above. The final price depends on the pages, content and features you need. We agree on the scope and price before work begins.

Will it work on mobile phones?

The website layout is designed to adapt to common phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes. The finished site should still be checked on the devices it needs to support.

Can customers contact me on WhatsApp?

Yes. The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message. The customer checks the message and presses Send in WhatsApp; this website does not send it automatically.

What information should I provide?

Your business name, services or products, contact details, preferred colours, and any logo or photos you have. We can discuss anything you do not have yet.

Is a domain included in the price?

Domain names, hosting and paid third-party services are not automatically included. Any extra costs will be discussed before you approve the project.

When will my website be ready?

The timeline depends on the website size, features and how quickly the content is provided. An estimated delivery date will be agreed before work begins.

Can I request changes?

The number and type of revisions will be agreed in the project scope before work begins. Extra changes beyond that scope may cost more.

Do I pay through this website?

No. This page does not collect payments. Project requirements, pricing and payment arrangements are discussed directly before work begins.

Clear expectations

Privacy and project terms.

Please review how enquiries work and what is agreed before a project starts.

Privacy: what happens to my enquiry?

This website has no account system or server-side enquiry database. When you submit the form, it prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. Your details are not automatically sent by this page; review the message and press Send yourself. If you send it, WhatsApp processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in the enquiry.

Project terms: what is included?

The prices shown are starting prices, not guaranteed final quotes. Before work starts, both sides should confirm the pages, features, content supplied by the customer, delivery estimate, number of revisions, payment milestones, domain/hosting costs, and any ongoing support in writing. Extra features or changes outside the agreed scope may cost extra. No search-engine ranking, sales result or specific business outcome is guaranteed.
